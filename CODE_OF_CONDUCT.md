# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Auto-Researcher is an open technical project, and we want collaboration here to feel respectful, practical, and safe.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful in discussion, review, and disagreement
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people
- assume good intent while still giving honest technical feedback
- welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- abusive, insulting, or demeaning language
- harassment, threats, or sustained hostility
- discriminatory remarks or exclusionary behavior
- publishing private information without permission
- spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing technical discussion

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, releases, and other project-managed communication channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, moderate, or remove content that violates this policy, and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community and the project.

## Reporting

For routine moderation concerns, open an issue or discussion with enough context for review.

If the matter is sensitive, open a minimal issue titled `[CoC] Request private contact` without including private details. The maintainer will move the conversation to a more appropriate channel.
